## How to evolve this corpus (per Constitution §9)

- **Adding a coordinate / declaring more** → minor amendment (always sound); bump within V1.x.
- **Removing/coarsening a coordinate, weakening an invariant, or changing an axiom** → **major** amendment; bump to V2.0, restate the migration, and re-derive every affected theorem and obligation.
- **Falsifying an engineering assumption [EA]** → identify every theorem/obligation that cited it and re-establish or retract.
- Any new governance document produced against this corpus is saved alongside these files at the `d:\lodstar` root.
